My name is Yingtai Xiao. I am a Research Scientist in the Privacy Innovation Lab at TikTok. Previously I earned my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering at The Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. [Daniel Kifer](https://www.cse.psu.edu/~duk17/) and Prof. [Danfeng Zhang](https://www.cse.psu.edu/~dbz5017/).

I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics from the University of Science and Technology of China, where I was supervised by Prof. [Juyong Zhang](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~juyong/index.html). I also visited Nanyang Technological University as a research assistant, under the supervision of Dr. [Yong Liu](http://www.yongliu.org/).

I'm interested in differential privacy and its applications in database query optimization, advertising measurement and machine learning. For a list of my publications, see [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=eCBNsH0AAAAJ&hl=en).
