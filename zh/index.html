TikTok 隐私创新实验室研究员

加利福尼亚州圣何塞

yingtai.xiao1@bytedance.com

我是 TikTok 隐私创新实验室的研究员，工作地点位于加利福尼亚州圣何塞。我于2024年5月在宾夕法尼亚州立大学获得计算机科学与工程博士学位，导师是 Daniel Kifer 教授 和 Danfeng Zhang 教授。

我的研究兴趣包括差分隐私、数据库查询优化、广告测量和机器学习。我特别关注开发可大规模部署的实用隐私保护系统。

selected publications

  1. VLDB
    Optimizing Fitness-For-Use of Differentially Private Linear Queries
    Yingtai Xiao, Daniel Kifer, and Danfeng Zhang
    In Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment, 2021
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

    Differential privacy has become the gold standard for statistical data release. A key challenge is to optimize the accuracy of released statistics while satisfying the privacy constraints. In this paper, we study the problem of optimizing the fitness-for-use of differentially private linear queries.

  2. CCS
    DPGen: Automated Program Synthesis for Differential Privacy
    Yingtai Xiao, Danfeng Zhang, and Daniel Kifer
    In Proceedings of the 2021 ACM SIGSAC Conference on Computer and Communications Security, 2021
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

    We present DPGen, a tool for automatically synthesizing differentially private programs. DPGen takes as input a non-private program and generates a differentially private version that achieves the desired privacy guarantee while optimizing utility.
