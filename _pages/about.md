I am a Research Scientist at [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/) Privacy Innovation Lab in San Jose, CA. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from [Penn State](https://www.psu.edu/) in May 2024, where I was advised by [Prof. Daniel Kifer](http://www.cse.psu.edu/~duk17/) and [Prof. Danfeng Zhang](http://www.cse.psu.edu/~dbz5017/).

My research interests lie in the intersection of **differential privacy**, **database query optimization**, **advertising measurement**, and **machine learning**. I am particularly interested in developing practical privacy-preserving systems that can be deployed at scale.
